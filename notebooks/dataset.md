# Datasets

## Dataset 1 — Indonesian Emotion Reviews
- **Size:** 5,400 samples, 5 classes
- **Source:** [Mendeley Data](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/574v66hf2v/1)
- **Citation:** Sutoyo, Rhio; Chowanda, Andry; Achmad, Said; Andangsari, Esther Widhi; M. Isa, Sani; Soetandar , Jason Pratama; Andres , Jomari Rasheed ; Richard , Richard ; Hadiwijaya , Michael Andrew ; Andrews , Darryl ; Pirdaus , Felix Putra (2022), “Product Reviews Dataset for Emotions Classification Tasks - Indonesian (PRDECT-ID) Dataset”, Mendeley Data, V1, doi: 10.17632/574v66hf2v.1

## Dataset 2 — Hoax/Valid News
- **Size:** 600 samples, 2 classes  
- **Source:** [Mendeley Data](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/p3hfgr5j3m/1)
- **Citation:** RAHUTOMO, FAISAL; YANUAR, INGGRID; ANDRIE ASMARA, ROSA (2018), “INDONESIAN HOAX NEWS DETECTION DATASET”, Mendeley Data, V1, doi: 10.17632/p3hfgr5j3m.1

## Dataset 3 — Medical Abstracts
- **Size:** 14,438 samples, 5 classes
- **Source:** [GitHub](https://github.com/sebischair/Medical-Abstracts-TC-Corpus)
- **Citation:** T. Schopf, D. Braun, and F. Matthes, “Evaluating Unsupervised Text Classification: Zero-shot and Similarity-based Approaches,” in Proceedings of the 2022 6th International Conference on Natural Language Processing and Information Retrieval, in NLPIR ’22. New York, NY, USA: Association for Computing Machinery, 2023, pp. 6–15. doi: 10.1145/3582768.3582795.
